Landmark-Based Gestures

Core Technology

Zoom In / Out

Point your index finger to zoom into an area of interest, or raise 4 fingers to zoom back out — instantly.

Brightness Control

Thumb up increases brightness continuously. Pinky up decreases it. Fine-tune any scan exposure with natural gestures.

Image Rotation

V-sign rotates the scan continuously. Hold the gesture to keep rotating, release to stop.

Grayscale Toggle

Open palm instantly toggles grayscale mode — essential for reading X-rays under different display conditions.

Gaussian Blur

3-finger gesture applies a Gaussian blur to reduce noise and focus on key anatomical structures.
